# Mini Assignment — Identify AI Around You

## Goal

Train your brain to recognize where AI is already being used in daily life.

## Task

Write down 5 digital products or apps you use regularly.

For each one, answer:

1. Does it use AI?
2. What kind of data does it likely use?
3. Is it AI, ML, or possibly Deep Learning?
4. What prediction or decision is the system making?

## Example

### Product
YouTube

### Does it use AI?
Yes.

### Data it may use
- watch history
- likes
- search behavior
- watch time
- skipped videos

### Type
AI + Machine Learning, possibly Deep Learning.

### Prediction
It predicts which videos you are most likely to watch next.

## Submission

Create a short note with your 5 examples.

You can write it in:
- Google Docs
- Notion
- Markdown file
- notebook text cell

## Reflection Question

Which AI system surprised you the most, and why?
