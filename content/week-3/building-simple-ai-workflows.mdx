# Building Simple AI Workflows

> A workflow turns AI from a one-time helper into a repeatable process.

## Lesson Goal

By the end of this lesson, you will understand how to combine multiple AI steps into a simple workflow.

Instead of asking one random prompt and hoping for a perfect answer, you will learn to guide AI through a clear sequence.

That sequence can be saved, reused, and improved.

---

## What Is An AI Workflow?

An AI workflow is a repeatable set of steps where AI helps you move from an input to a useful output.

For example:

```text
Idea -> research -> outline -> draft -> improve -> final review
```

Each step has a job.

The workflow gives the AI structure.

It also gives you more control.

---

## Why Workflows Beat Random Prompting

Random prompting often looks like this:

> Write me a post about productivity.

The AI may give you something usable, but it has to guess:

- who the post is for
- what angle matters
- what format you want
- what examples to include
- what quality level you expect

A workflow breaks the task into smaller steps.

That usually creates better output because each step is easier to guide.

---

## Input, Process, Output

A simple workflow has three parts.

### Input

This is what you start with.

Examples:

- an idea
- a topic
- a goal
- a question
- rough notes

### Process

This is the sequence of steps.

Examples:

- research the topic
- organize the points
- create an outline
- write a first draft
- improve the draft

### Output

This is the useful thing you want at the end.

Examples:

- a post
- a study note
- a script
- a checklist
- a plan

When your input, process, and output are clear, AI becomes easier to use.

---

## Example Workflow

Here is a simple content workflow.

### Step 1: Idea

Start with one clear topic.

> I want to create content about how beginners can use AI to study better.

### Step 2: Research

Ask AI to explain the topic, list key points, and identify common beginner questions.

### Step 3: Outline

Ask AI to organize the best points into a simple structure.

### Step 4: Draft

Ask AI to write the first version in a clear style.

### Step 5: Improve

Ask AI to make the draft more specific, useful, and easy to understand.

### Step 6: Human Review

You check the facts, remove weak lines, and add your own examples.

The AI helps with the process, but you still guide the final result.

---

## Saving And Reusing Workflows

A workflow becomes more valuable when you save it.

You can save:

- the steps
- the prompts for each step
- the expected output format
- notes on what worked well

The next time you do a similar task, you do not start from zero.

You reuse the workflow and improve it.

---

## Mini Exercise: Design A 3-Step Workflow

Choose one simple task.

Examples:

- plan a study session
- write a short post
- summarize a lesson
- create a weekly schedule

Now write three steps:

```text
Step 1: Input
Step 2: Process
Step 3: Output
```

For example:

```text
Step 1: Give AI my lesson notes.
Step 2: Ask AI to organize the notes into key ideas and questions.
Step 3: Ask AI to create a short study checklist.
```

Keep it simple.

A useful workflow is better than a complicated one.
