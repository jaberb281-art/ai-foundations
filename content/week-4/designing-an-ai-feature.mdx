# Designing an AI Feature

> A good AI feature helps a person complete one specific task better, faster, or more clearly.

## Lesson Goal

By the end of this lesson, you will know how to describe one useful AI feature inside a simple product.

You are not trying to design a giant app.

You are learning how to choose one helpful AI-powered action.

---

## What Is An AI Feature?

An AI feature is a part of a product that uses AI to help the user do something.

Examples:

- rewrite a resume bullet point
- summarize long notes
- create a study plan
- suggest better email wording
- classify customer messages
- turn rough ideas into an outline

The feature should not be "AI exists here."

The feature should help with a task the user already cares about.

---

## Input, Process, Output

A simple AI feature has three parts.

### Input

What does the user give the product?

This could be text, a goal, a file, a topic, answers to questions, or a set of preferences.

### Process

What does the AI do with that input?

It may summarize, rewrite, compare, classify, generate, explain, plan, or suggest.

### Output

What does the user receive?

This should be something useful, clear, and easy to act on.

If you cannot describe the input and output, the feature is probably too vague.

---

## AI Should Help With A Specific Task

Avoid saying:

> The AI helps users with everything.

That sounds powerful, but it is hard to design.

Instead, say:

> The AI helps students turn rough experience notes into resume bullet points.

This is specific.

It tells you what the user provides and what the product returns.

Specific features are easier to build, test, explain, and improve.

---

## Avoiding "AI For Everything"

Beginners often try to build a product that does too much.

For example:

- AI resume writer
- AI career coach
- AI job search assistant
- AI interview trainer
- AI networking helper
- AI portfolio builder

All in one product.

That is too much for a first version.

A better first feature:

> Improve one resume bullet point for clarity and impact.

Small does not mean weak.

Small means focused.

---

## Example: AI Resume Improvement Helper

Problem:

Students struggle to describe their experience clearly on a resume.

Feature:

An AI resume improvement helper.

Input:

- the student's rough bullet point
- the role they are applying for
- the skill they want to highlight

Process:

- rewrite the bullet point clearly
- make it action-focused
- suggest stronger wording
- keep it honest and beginner-friendly

Output:

- three improved bullet point options
- one short explanation of why each option is stronger

This feature is useful because it solves one part of the bigger resume problem.

---

## Feature Design Template

Use this structure:

1. User: Who uses this feature?
2. Problem: What task are they struggling with?
3. Input: What does the user provide?
4. AI process: What should AI do?
5. Output: What does the user receive?
6. Quality rule: What makes the output good?

Example quality rules:

- simple language
- honest wording
- clear next steps
- specific suggestions
- no fake achievements
